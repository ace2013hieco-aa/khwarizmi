# Contributing

Contributions are accepted under the Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`).
Every commit must carry a Developer Certificate of Origin sign-off trailer:

```
Signed-off-by: Your Name <you@example.com>
```

The sign-off certifies that you have the right to submit the contribution
under the project licence (DCO 1.1, https://developercertificate.org/).
There is no separate Contributor Licence Agreement.

Third-party material keeps its own licence and attribution; see `NOTICE`
and `docs/ASSET-PROVENANCE.md`.
